Fairness Metrics

Three standard tests, applied to the denial rate of genuine visits:

  • Disparity ratio: a group's denial rate divided by the best group's rate (1.0 = equal).
  • Four-fifths rule, adapted to failures: the classic rule flags a group whose success rate is below 80% of the best group's. For failure rates we flip it: a group FAILS if its denial rate is more than {{ "%.2f"|format(limit) }}x (= 1 / 0.8) the best group's.
  • Chi-square test: is denial independent of the attribute? A p-value below {{ alpha }} means the differences are unlikely to be chance.

The best (reference) group must have at least {{ min_group }} visits so a tiny group cannot set the benchmark.
