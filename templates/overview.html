How one visit works

  1. ScanThe device captures a fingerprint or iris and scores its quality from 0 to 100.
  2. MatchThe scan is compared with the stored template. Below the threshold, it is rejected.
  3. RetryUp to three attempts are allowed in one visit.
  4. OutcomeIf every attempt fails, the service is denied for that visit.
